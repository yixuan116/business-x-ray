Where did the margin go?

The mix shifted. P1 (60% margin) went from 80% to 60% of revenue. P2 (10% margin) grew from 20% to 40%. Revenue grew 25% to $1,000K, but profit stayed flat at $400K as blended margin compressed from 50% to 40%.

Break down the revenue drivers.

P1 raised price 50% but lost volume (8 to 5 units). P2 raised price too, and field sales doubled volume (5 to 10). Revenue grew from 800K to 1,000K, but the extra revenue landed in the lower-margin product, so profit stayed flat.

What can we do about it?

Two levers. A: switch sales commission from revenue-based to profit-based. Reps push P1, mix returns to 80/20, profit rises to $500K. B: cut P1 price to win volume back, raise P2 price to compress demand. Same result, different trade-off. A changes internal incentives. B changes what the customer sees.
